# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are nature, adventure, culture, and heritage travellers considering a remote Borneo visit. They are usually discovering Long Taa on a phone and need enough practical confidence to decide whether the experience suits them before beginning a booking conversation.

Secondary audiences include eco-tourists, photographers, researchers, students, remote workers, families, and small groups who value authentic community stays over conventional resort tourism.

## Product Purpose

Long Taa Borneo Eco Stay gives a living Sebup longhouse community one clear digital home for its story, experiences, practical rates, travel conditions, and booking path. Success means visitors quickly understand what Long Taa is, arrive with appropriate expectations, and send a useful availability enquiry to Clement Langet through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Long Taa is a real, community-led Sebup village experience approximately six hours from Miri—not a purpose-built luxury resort. Its distinct value is the connection of rainforest, Dapui River, longhouse life, conservation, adventure, and living heritage within one community landscape.

## Operating Context

The website supports a five-stage visitor journey: discover Long Taa, understand stay options and costs, explore the landscape, learn the community context, and plan a visit. Booking remains a human conversation because accommodation, transport, guides, activities, road access, weather, river level, and safety conditions require local confirmation.

The authoritative business sources are the supplied overview, brochure, logo, and photography under `docs/`. The public submission also includes a pitch deck and process video, but the design demos in `demohtml/` are exploratory alternatives rather than production replacements.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five existing website routes: Home, Stay & Experiences, Explore, Our Story, and Plan & Book.
- Confirmed published rates: RM50 per person/night for accommodation only; RM180 per person/night with breakfast, lunch, and local dinner; RM1,500 per return 4WD vehicle; RM600 per longboat group with local guide and porter.
- Maximum three guests per 4WD vehicle and longboat; no minimum guest count.
- Calls to action open a prefilled WhatsApp enquiry to `+60 19-856 3536`; an enquiry is never represented as a confirmed booking.
- Package-specific totals, availability, guarantees, testimonials, visitor metrics, and environmental-impact metrics must not be invented.
- Activities must remain qualified by weather, road, river, water-level, availability, and safety conditions.
- Exact map pins, pickup instructions, detailed booking policies, accessibility claims, and unapproved cultural protocols remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Long Taa Borneo Eco Stay.
- Core line: “Escape the city. Experience the real Borneo.”
- Supporting line: “Come as a visitor. Leave with a story.”
- Brand pillars: Nature, Culture, Adventure, Living Heritage.
- Preserve the supplied Long Taa logo and use authentic supplied imagery respectfully.
- Voice is grounded, welcoming, practical, and culturally respectful. Avoid exoticising language and luxury-resort positioning.
- “Mostar Guide,” “JungleMind,” and “Aethera Studio” are user-requested exploratory visual references for three separate demos, not approved permanent brand systems.

## Product Principles

1. Community ownership leads the story.
2. Desire and practical clarity work together.
3. Authentic photographs carry more authority than invented imagery.
4. Conditions and uncertainty remain visible rather than hidden in fine print.
5. Every strong call to action starts a qualified conversation, not a false promise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, readable at narrow mobile widths, and compatible with reduced-motion preferences. Text over photography requires reliable contrast, touch targets should meet WCAG 2.2 minimum guidance, and imagery needs meaningful alternative text. Detailed physical-accessibility and child-suitability claims require beneficiary confirmation.
