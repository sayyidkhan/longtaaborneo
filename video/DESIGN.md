# Long Taa Process Video Design

## Style Prompt

A bold, documentary-editorial vertical process film for a community-led Borneo eco-stay. It should feel like field notes becoming a usable visitor journey: warm paper surfaces, deep rainforest shadows, river-green movement, honest supplied photography, and precise booking information. The build process is the story, not an invented outcome. Use organic vertical pushes and soft dissolves to suggest travel into the interior, with a single energetic editorial switch when the five-page solution is revealed.

## Colours

- `#102D25` — deep rainforest base
- `#1E6454` — river green accent
- `#E9E1D0` — warm paper / primary light text
- `#C77A37` — clay highlight
- `#151917` — charcoal ink

## Typography

- Display: `Georgia` with `DejaVu Serif` fallback, a restrained editorial serif for destination statements.
- Functional/captions: `Liberation Mono`, a precise build-log voice for facts and process markers.

## Motion

- Mobile-native editorial pacing: 0.5–0.8 second documentary entrances and 0.35–0.5 second vertical-push transitions.
- Captions enter one phrase at a time in the lower middle and are never placed over identifiable faces.
- Keep the closing card calm and readable. Honour reduced visual complexity by avoiding flicker, glitch, or frantic cuts.

## What Not To Do

- Do not depict Long Taa as a luxury resort or promise an experience, sighting, price, or availability not confirmed by the supplied materials.
- Do not use generic tropical stock imagery, AI people, or altered community/cultural imagery.
- Do not use neon gradients, glassmorphism, generic travel-template cards, or UI clutter.
- Do not turn community members into decorative visual props.
