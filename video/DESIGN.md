# Long Taa Process Video Design

## Style Prompt

A bold, documentary-editorial vertical tourism film for a community-led Borneo eco-stay. Open with a short, premium heritage-house reveal of the authentic Long Taa crest: deep rainforest green, restrained clay-gold star trails, concentric fine-line rings, and one polished light sweep. Follow it with a playful fourth-wall hook: the narrator makes a bold destination claim, Sayyid challenges it from a bottom-right picture-in-picture window, and a compact eyebrow-raise reaction answers from the opposite corner. Then earn the claim with authentic Long Taa experiences before showing how one clear digital journey helps travellers understand the trip and plan with confidence. Use warm paper surfaces, deep rainforest shadows, river-green movement, honest supplied photography, and precise booking information. Organic vertical pushes suggest travel into the interior; a faster contact-sheet montage sells the experience; the real mobile website lands later as the trusted planning companion.

## Colours

- `#102D25` — deep rainforest base
- `#1E6454` — river green accent
- `#E9E1D0` — warm paper / primary light text
- `#C77A37` — clay highlight
- `#151917` — charcoal ink

## Typography

- Display: `Georgia` with `DejaVu Serif` fallback, a restrained editorial serif for destination statements.
- Functional/captions: `Liberation Mono`, a precise build-log voice for facts and process markers.

## Motion

- First 2.6 seconds: an elegant logo scale-up, outward heritage rings, four directional star trails, small controlled sparkles, and a single light sweep; premium and cinematic rather than flashy.
- Mobile-native editorial pacing: 0.5–0.8 second documentary entrances and 0.35–0.5 second vertical-push transitions.
- Captions enter as large editorial statements or small source-backed labels and are never placed over identifiable faces.
- Keep the closing card calm and readable. Honour reduced visual complexity by avoiding flicker, glitch, or frantic cuts.
- Presenter inserts use warm-paper frames, clay corner marks and deep forest shadows. They should feel like editorial windows cut into the film, not social-media stickers.
- The reaction beat uses a hard comedic pause: Sayyid speaks first, then the eyebrow-raise sting lands. Jessie is silent during the entire interruption.

## What Not To Do

- Do not depict Long Taa as a luxury resort or promise an experience, sighting, price, or availability not confirmed by the supplied materials.
- Do not use generic tropical stock imagery, AI people, or altered community/cultural imagery.
- Do not use neon gradients, glassmorphism, generic travel-template cards, or UI clutter.
- Do not turn community members into decorative visual props.
- Do not let the reaction meme dominate the destination, repeat it, or continue beyond the opening joke.
