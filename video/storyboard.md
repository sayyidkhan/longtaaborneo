## Picture-in-Picture Treatment

- Sayyid's windows are anchored bottom-right with a warm-paper border, clay corner marker and deep rainforest shadow.
- The reaction meme is smaller and bottom-left so it supports the joke without competing with the host.
- Both inserts enter with a quick scale-and-rise and leave on a clean cut; no sticker outlines, speech bubbles or novelty emojis.
- The destination remains visible behind the reaction beat so the joke never disconnects from Long Taa.

## Accuracy Guardrails

- “Best” is presented as an opening opinion and immediately challenged on screen; it is not supported with an invented award or ranking.
- Long Taa is presented as a living Sebup village, not a luxury resort or staged attraction.
- Only confirmed rates appear: RM50, RM180, RM1,500 and RM600.
- Activities remain subject to weather, road, river, water-level, availability and safety conditions.
- The website supports better-qualified conversations; it does not claim measured bookings, conversion or revenue results.
- The WhatsApp flow is an enquiry for availability and quotation, not an instant booking confirmation.
- Community and cultural imagery remains sourced from supplied project material and is not altered into spectacle.
