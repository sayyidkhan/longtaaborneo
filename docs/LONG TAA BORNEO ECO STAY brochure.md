Long Taa, Dapui, Ulu Tinjar, Baram, Sarawak, Malaysia

Leave the noise and rush of the city behind and experience the authentic life of interior Borneo.

Stay in a Sebup longhouse, journey along the Dapui River, explore the rainforest, discover community-led conservation, and experience a Living Heritage Village where culture, adventure, nature and traditional ways of life are still very much alive.

Come as a visitor. Leave with a story.

## 🌿 CHOOSE YOUR EXPERIENCE

### PACKAGE 1

## RIVER, CULTURE & LIVING HERITAGE EXPERIENCE

Discover the heart of Long Taa through its river, longhouse, people, wildlife, culture and community conservation.

#### 🚤 Dapui River Adventure

Cruise along the beautiful Dapui River by traditional longboat, swim in its refreshing waters and enjoy the rainforest scenery along the river.

#### 🐟 Tagang Fish Conservation

Visit the community-managed Long Taa Tagang Fish Conservation Area and see native freshwater fish such as Semah, Empurau, Sultan, Adong and Kaloi, subject to river and water-level conditions.

Experience the excitement of feeding these fish while learning how the local community protects and restores the Dapui River ecosystem.

#### 🏡 Sebup Longhouse Experience

Stay in a Sebup longhouse and experience everyday community life in Long Taa.

Discover a way of life rarely experienced in conventional tourism — simple, authentic and closely connected to nature.

#### 🌱 Living Heritage Experience

Discover how the Sebup community continues to protect and practise its language, culture, traditional knowledge, river conservation, forest stewardship and wildlife protection as part of everyday life.

Here, culture is not simply displayed for visitors.

It is lived.

#### 🦜 Wildlife & Rainforest Experience

Explore the natural surroundings and look or listen for hornbills, monkeys and other rainforest wildlife in their natural habitat.

When night falls, listen to the sounds of insects and nocturnal wildlife while experiencing something increasingly rare in modern life:

the true silence of the Borneo interior.

No city traffic.
No crowds.
Just the sounds of nature.

---

### PACKAGE 2

## NATURE’S WONDERS EXPLORATION

For travellers who love exploration, trekking, natural formations and discovering places far beyond the usual tourist trail.

#### 💧 Acin Salt Spring

Visit a natural salt spring hidden in the rainforest, known locally as Acin. The mineral-rich spring also attracts wildlife from the surrounding forest.

#### ⛰️ Batu Ukat – Ladder Rock

Explore Batu Ukat, a unique natural ladder-like rock formation and one of Long Taa’s distinctive wilderness attractions.

#### 🪨 Batu Nginan

Discover Batu Nginan, a fascinating gigantic natural rock formation hidden within the Dapui landscape.

#### 🌿 Batu Tatip

Experience the adventure of squeezing through the narrow natural passage of Batu Tatip, a unique rock formation surrounded by local stories.

#### 🚪 Batu Belacek – The Rock Door

Discover Batu Belacek, an unusual natural rock formation resembling a doorway hidden within the Borneo wilderness.
