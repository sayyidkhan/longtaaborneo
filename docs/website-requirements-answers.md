# Website Requirements — Answers and Beneficiary Confirmations

> **Companion document:** `website-requirements-document.md`
> **Prepared from:** Supplied Long Taa overview, brochure, images, logo, and project research
> **Purpose:** Answer the ten questions in the requirements document without inventing business facts
> **Status key:** **Confirmed** = stated in supplied materials; **Recommended** = proposed website decision; **Needs confirmation** = beneficiary input required before publication

## 1. Are all supplied rates current, and how should the website explain the cost of Package 1 and Package 2?

### Current answer

The supplied materials state these rates:

- Accommodation only: **RM50 per person per night**.
- Accommodation with local meals: **RM180 per person per night**, including breakfast, lunch, and a local dinner.
- Return 4WD transfer from Miri to Long Taa: **RM1,500 per vehicle**, with a maximum of three guests per vehicle.
- Longboat, local guide, and porter: **RM600 per group**, with a maximum of three guests per longboat.
- No minimum number of guests is required.
- Larger groups may be arranged using additional vehicles and longboats, subject to availability.

**Confirmed:** These figures appear in the supplied brochure.
**Needs confirmation:** The beneficiary must confirm that the figures are still current.

No separate total price is supplied for Package 1 or Package 2. The website should describe the activities in each package but must not invent a package price or imply that the itemised rates form a complete package total.

**Recommended website wording:**

> Experience pricing is arranged according to your selected stay, transport, group size, and activities. Contact Long Taa on WhatsApp for availability and a confirmed quotation.

## 2. Does the 3 Days 2 Nights duration apply to both packages and all booking options?

### Current answer

The brochure is headed **“3 Days 2 Nights – Nature’s Wonders Adventure”** and later presents both Package 1 and Package 2. However, it does not explicitly state whether every package and booking option must use that duration.

**Needs confirmation:** Confirm whether three days and two nights applies to:

- Package 1 only;
- Package 2 only;
- both packages;
- a recommended itinerary rather than a fixed duration; or
- all stays booked through Long Taa.

Until confirmed, the website may mention that the supplied brochure features a three-day, two-night experience, but it should not label every booking option with that duration.

## 3. What booking lead time, deposit, payment methods, cancellation terms, and contact hours should be published?

### Current answer

**Confirmed:** Advance booking is required. Final confirmation is subject to the availability of accommodation, transportation, and local guides. Activities may change, be postponed, or be replaced because of weather, river, water-level, road, or safety conditions.

**Needs confirmation:** The supplied materials do not state:

- minimum booking lead time;
- deposit amount or percentage;
- accepted payment methods;
- payment deadline;
- cancellation, refund, or rescheduling terms;
- preferred WhatsApp contact hours;
- expected response time.

Until these are confirmed, the website should direct visitors to WhatsApp to check availability and must not promise that an enquiry is a confirmed reservation.

## 4. May an exact map pin, pickup point, and travel route be published?

### Current answer

**Confirmed:**

- Destination: Long Taa, Dapui, Ulu Tinjar, Baram, Sarawak, Malaysia.
- Starting point: Miri, Sarawak.
- Journey: approximately six hours by 4WD.

**Needs confirmation:** No approved coordinates, map pin, Miri pickup point, detailed route, or public arrival instructions were supplied.

## 5. What facilities, sleeping arrangements, dietary support, packing advice, accessibility details, and child suitability can be stated?

### Current answer

**Confirmed facilities and inclusions:**

- Basic longhouse accommodation in a living Sebup community.
- 24-hour solar electricity.
- Fresh mountain-sourced water.
- Telecommunications connectivity.
- Self-cooking facilities for accommodation-only guests.
- Breakfast, lunch, and local dinner under the accommodation-with-meals option.
- Personal trekking and outdoor equipment, medication, travel insurance, and personal expenses are not included.
- The experience is presented as suitable for families and small groups, but no specific age or child-safety guidance is supplied.

**Needs confirmation:**

- bed and room configuration;
- room privacy and capacity;
- bathroom and toilet arrangements;
- shower and hot-water availability;
- charging sockets and electrical limits;
- mobile-network reliability;
- dietary requirements, allergies, vegetarian, halal, or other meal support;
- recommended clothing, footwear, rain protection, insect protection, and equipment;
- physical fitness requirements;
- accessibility for visitors with reduced mobility or other disabilities;
- minimum age, child pricing, supervision, and activity suitability;
- medical, emergency, and evacuation guidance.

The website must avoid assumptions about these items until the beneficiary confirms them.

## 6. Which visitor etiquette, photography, cultural protocols, and restricted-area guidance should be published?

### Current answer

**Confirmed:** Visitors are expected to respect Long Taa’s conservation areas, Sebup customs and culture, and community rules.

**Recommended interim principles:**

- Remember that Long Taa is a living village, not a staged attraction.
- Follow instructions from hosts and local guides.
- Respect conservation zones and do not disturb wildlife or remove natural materials.
- Ask permission before photographing or recording identifiable people, homes, ceremonies, or culturally sensitive places.
- Do not enter private or restricted areas without permission.
- Keep noise and waste to a minimum.
- Do not publish detailed cultural knowledge or locations without community consent.

**Needs confirmation:** The community should approve the final wording, photography rules, any restricted locations, dress or behaviour expectations, and any knowledge or ceremonies that must not be recorded or shared.

## 7. Are all supplied images and the logo approved for public website use?

### Current answer

The project materials include a logo and authentic Long Taa photographs, including photographs containing identifiable people.

**Needs confirmation:** File possession does not prove permission for unrestricted public or promotional use. The beneficiary should confirm:

- that Long Taa owns the assets or has permission to use them;
- which images are approved for the public website, pitch deck, and process video;
- whether identifiable people have consented to public and promotional use;
- whether credit lines are required;
- whether any images show private, sacred, sensitive, or restricted subjects;
- whether images may be cropped, colour-adjusted, animated, or used behind text.

Until confirmed, prioritise landscape, river, rainforest, longhouse, and non-identifying images. Do not use AI-generated people as substitutes for the community.

## 8. What are the approved brand colours, logo versions, and brand rules?

### Current answer

**Confirmed:** A logo is included in the supplied project materials.

**Needs confirmation:** No formal brand guide, colour values, clear-space rules, minimum logo size, alternate logo variants, or usage restrictions were supplied.

**Recommended temporary design system:**

- Deep rainforest green as the primary colour.
- Muted river teal or blue as the secondary colour.
- Warm earth as the accent colour.
- Warm off-white as the primary background.
- High-contrast dark text for practical information.
- Editorial display typography paired with a legible sans-serif.
- Authentic photography, restrained motion, and no luxury-resort styling.

The production team may sample provisional colours from the supplied logo and photographs. Record the selected colour values in the website design tokens and label them as provisional until approved.

## 10. Which WhatsApp enquiry fields would be most useful?

### Recommended answer

Keep the form short enough for mobile users while collecting the information needed to assess availability and estimate logistics.

**Required fields:**

1. Name.
2. Preferred arrival date.
3. Number of guests.
4. Number of nights or preferred trip duration.
5. Stay option: accommodation only or accommodation with local meals.
6. Experience interest: Package 1, Package 2, both, or undecided.
7. Transport needed from Miri: yes, no, or discuss.

**Optional fields:**

- dietary or accessibility needs;
- children in the group and their ages;
- questions or special requests.

The form should generate a prefilled WhatsApp message to **+60 19-856 3536**. It should say **“Send booking enquiry”**, not **“Confirm booking”**.

**Needs confirmation:** Ask Clement Langet whether any fields should be added or removed based on how bookings are currently handled.
