## 2. Business Challenge

Long Taa offers a distinctive authentic Borneo experience, but the supplied information is distributed across brochures and social channels. The destination’s remoteness makes clear planning information especially important: visitors need to understand the setting, accommodation style, travel from Miri, experiences, conditions, and how to enquire.

A website can create one trustworthy, shareable place for this information. It should build confidence without overstating what Long Taa is: a living village rather than a purpose-built luxury resort. Its role is to turn interest into well-informed WhatsApp booking conversations.

## 3. Website Objectives

**Primary objective**

Move qualified visitors to start a booking enquiry on WhatsApp.

**Secondary objectives**

- Explain the authentic longhouse, nature, culture, adventure, and living-heritage offer quickly.
- Set realistic expectations about simple accommodation, the remote journey, advance booking, and condition-dependent activities.
- Help visitors compare accommodation and experience options before contacting the business.
- Present community and conservation values respectfully.
- Make the destination easy to share from mobile devices.

**Desired customer actions**

1. Explore Long Taa and determine whether the experience suits them.
2. Review stay options, package inclusions, and practical conditions.
3. Check travel guidance and prepare relevant booking details.
4. Tap **Book on WhatsApp** to check dates, discuss an experience, or make a booking.

## 9. Page 2 — Stay & Experiences

**Purpose:** Help visitors understand the available stay and experience options before they enquire.

**Recommended content**

- **Accommodation only — RM50 per person / night:** longhouse accommodation; guests prepare their own meals; meals are not included.
- **Accommodation + local meals — RM180 per person / night:** accommodation, breakfast, lunch, and local dinner.
- **Package 1 — River, Culture & Living Heritage Experience:** Dapui River adventure, Tagang Fish Conservation, Sebup longhouse experience, living heritage, wildlife, and rainforest experience.

## 10. Page 3 — Explore Long Taa

**Purpose:** Create destination desire with authentic visual storytelling, while avoiding promises that conditions may not permit.

**Section structure**

1. Hero: **Nature beyond the usual tourist trail.**
2. Dapui River and rainforest introduction.
3. River, wildlife, and conservation highlights.
4. Natural-wonders trail: Acin Salt Spring and the named rock formations.
5. Responsive photo gallery.
6. Conditions notice and CTA.

**Recommended content and headlines**

- **The Dapui River:** Longboat journeys, river scenery, and swimming in refreshing waters where appropriate.
- **A river protected by its community:** Introduce the Long Taa Tagang Fish Conservation Area and the native fish named in the brochure, noting that viewing is subject to river and water-level conditions.
- **Rainforest, wildlife, and stillness:** Explain that visitors may look or listen for rainforest wildlife such as hornbills and monkeys; do not guarantee sightings.
- **Natural wonders of the Dapui landscape:** Present Acin Salt Spring, Batu Ukat (Ladder Rock), Batu Nginan, Batu Tatip, and Batu Belacek (The Rock Door) with factual, concise captions.
- **Conditions matter:** Clearly state that activities are subject to weather, river, water-level, road, and safety conditions.

**CTAs:** `See Stay & Experiences`; `Ask About Your Preferred Experience` (WhatsApp).

**Suggested visuals:** Supplied landscape, river, forest, and rock-formation photography. Use a keyboard- and touch-accessible lightbox gallery, with meaningful alt text.

## 18. Mobile Experience

- Prioritise a clear `Book on WhatsApp` action without covering essential controls or form fields.
- Keep navigation thumb-friendly; menu links and buttons must be easy to tap.
- Use one-column content flows, readable body text, and high contrast over image surfaces.
- Serve correctly sized, compressed real images; avoid heavy autoplay video and large 3D effects on mobile.
- Preserve image subjects when crops change from desktop to mobile.
- Keep forms short, labelled, and easy to complete; use appropriate mobile input types for phone numbers and dates if added.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain native scrolling behaviour.
- Test key routes on common narrow screens: navigation, gallery, WhatsApp CTA, and enquiry form.
