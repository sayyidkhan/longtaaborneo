## Core Business Problem

Long Taa offers an authentic Indigenous Borneo experience, but its remote location creates discovery, trust, planning, and booking friction. Prospective visitors need to understand what makes the experience special, what the journey involves, what each option costs, what is included, and how to book.

The website should turn scattered brochure material into a clear visitor journey that builds desire, reduces uncertainty, and moves qualified visitors into a WhatsApp booking conversation.

## Recommended Positioning

**Escape the city. Experience the real Borneo.**

Long Taa is not a luxury resort. It is a living Sebup village offering an authentic combination of nature, culture, adventure, conservation, and living heritage.

Primary audience:

- Experience-driven nature, culture, and adventure travellers
- Especially groups of up to three who can plan in advance
- Visitors comfortable with a remote six-hour 4WD journey and simple longhouse accommodation
- Respectful travellers seeking a living community experience rather than a luxury resort

Secondary audiences include eco-tourists, photographers, researchers, students, families, and remote workers. Family suitability and longer-stay working needs must be checked with Clement.

Recommended launch market: start with Miri- and Sarawak-based groups of up to three, then expand to fly-in eco-adventure travellers from Brunei, Singapore, wider Southeast Asia, and international visitors already planning a Miri or Sarawak trip.

## Recommended Five-Page Website

### 1. Home — Experience the Real Borneo

- Cinematic hero using authentic Long Taa imagery
- Clear positioning and trust-building introduction
- Short journey from Miri to Long Taa
- Highlights of nature, culture, adventure, and living heritage
- Strong `Plan Your Trip` and `Request Availability on WhatsApp` calls to action

### 2. Stay & Packages

- Accommodation-only option: RM50 per person per night
- Accommodation plus local meals: RM180 per person per night
- Ask for preferred date, guests, and nights; suggest 3 Days 2 Nights while allowing other durations
- Require one experience choice: Package 1 or Package 2, not both
- Default to Long Taa's return 4WD; own transport requires a terrain-suitable 4WD and prior approval
- Ask whether river/activity support is required
- Included and excluded items
- Itemised indicative estimator for stay, 4WD transfer, and river/activity support
- Clearly exclude unconfirmed package/activity costs and send all assumptions to WhatsApp

### 3. Explore Long Taa

- Dapui River adventure
- Tagang Fish Conservation Area
- Rainforest and wildlife
- Acin Salt Spring
- Batu Ukat, Batu Nginan, Batu Tatip, and Batu Belacek
- Authentic photo gallery

### 4. Our Story & Living Heritage

- Long Taa and the Indigenous Sebup community
- Community-based conservation
- Language, culture, traditional knowledge, and forest stewardship
- Visitor etiquette and responsible-tourism principles
- Clear statement that this is a living village, not a purpose-built resort

### 5. Plan & Book

- Starting point: Miri, Sarawak
- Approximate six-hour 4WD journey
- Long Taa return 4WD as the default transport arrangement
- Own terrain-suitable 4WD as an approval request—not ordinary self-drive access
- Transport and longboat rates
- Weather, road, river, water-level, and safety conditions
- FAQ and packing guidance
- Contact details
- Trip-planning form that shows an indicative estimate and generates a prefilled WhatsApp availability-and-quotation request

## Website Experience Direction

Use an editorial, cinematic rainforest style built from the supplied real photography. The experience should feel premium while remaining truthful about basic longhouse accommodation.

Recommended resource usage:

- **MotionSites AI:** use one compressed cinematic hero loop or subtle moving image. Avoid video backgrounds across the whole site because of load-time risk.
- **Originkit:** use selected production-ready components for the hero, gallery, text reveal, or CTA. Do not assemble the whole site from unrelated effects.
- **Scroll Craft:** use scroll as a narrative timeline on the home page. Create one emotional peak, such as the reveal of Long Taa after the journey from Miri.
- **Image to 3JS:** optional. Only use a procedural 3D element if it supports the story, such as a subtle Borneo terrain or journey map. Do not let experimental 3D delay the working submission.
- **ThreeUI Living Green:** use as forest-themed visual inspiration, not as a copied template.
- **Podium:** borrow its full-bleed imagery, disciplined typography, and story-led pacing.
- **Lenis:** use for controlled smooth scrolling with native-scroll and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- **Rive:** optional for a small logo or route animation. Skip it unless the interaction materially improves the demo.
