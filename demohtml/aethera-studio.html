A living village, not a tourist resort

Long Taa is a traditional 20-door longhouse and home of the Indigenous Sebup community in Ulu Tinjar, Baram.

Come for the rainforest. Understand the life within it.

Simple living. Deep context.

Stay inside the longhouse and experience everyday rural life rather than conventional hotel tourism. The accommodation is basic; the invitation is to slow down, listen and learn.

Guests retain essential conveniences: 24-hour solar electricity, fresh mountain-sourced water and telecommunications connectivity.

Protect nature. Respect culture. Share heritage.

Move at the pace of the place.

River, rainforest, longhouse life and living heritage meet within one community landscape. Activities are arranged with local guidance and depend on conditions.
