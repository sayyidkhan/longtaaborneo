A living village

Not built for tourism. Shared with visitors.

Long Taa, Dapui is a traditional 20-door longhouse and home of the Indigenous Sebup community. This is everyday community life—not a purpose-built resort.

6happroximately, by 4WD from Miri

Stay close to the story

Simple longhouse living.

Wake to birds and rainforest sounds. Spend the day by the river or exploring the forest. The accommodation is basic, grounded in the rhythm of a real village.

Culture is not displayed here. It is lived.

Visit as a guest of a living Sebup community. Follow your hosts and local guides, respect conservation areas, and ask before photographing people, homes or culturally sensitive places.
