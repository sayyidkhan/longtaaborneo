A living village, not a tourist set.

Long Taa Borneo Eco Stay was created to share the natural beauty, Sebup culture and traditional way of life of Long Taa, Dapui while supporting community-based conservation and sustainable rural development.

Culture is not placed behind glass here. It remains connected to the people, river, forest and knowledge that sustain it. Visitors enter that context as guests.

Protect nature.
Respect culture.
Share heritage. Follow your hosts and local guides, respect conservation areas and community rules, and ask before photographing people or private spaces.

Borneo beyond the ordinary.

Dapui River

Traditional longboat journeys, river exploration and swimming in clear water, shaped by local guidance.

Forest routes

Jungle trekking and exploration around Acin Salt Spring and distinctive rock formations including Batu Ukat, Batu Nginan, Batu Tatip and Batu Belacek.

Longhouse life

Simple accommodation in the longhouse, with the rhythms and hospitality of everyday community life.

Living heritage

Discover how language, culture, traditional knowledge, river conservation and forest stewardship continue to be practised.
