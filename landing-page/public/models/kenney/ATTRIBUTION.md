# 3D model sources

- `tree-oak.glb`: [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit)
- `structure.glb` and `Textures/colormap.png`: [Kenney Survival Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit)
