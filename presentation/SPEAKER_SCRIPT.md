# Long Taa Borneo Eco Stay - Three-Minute Pitch Script

**Target duration:** 169 seconds at a natural presentation pace.
**Deck:** `exports/long-taa-borneo-pitch.pptx` and `exports/long-taa-borneo-pitch.pdf`.

## Slide 1 - Hook (0:00-0:15, 15s)

Six hours from Miri, a world away from the ordinary. Long Taa Borneo Eco Stay is a real Sebup longhouse village in Ulu Tinjar, Baram, where visitors can experience rainforest, river, adventure, and living heritage. The opportunity is compelling, but the journey into it was not clear enough online.

## Slide 2 - The challenge (0:15-0:40, 25s)

Long Taa is distinctive, but remote. A potential visitor had to piece together brochures and social posts to answer basic questions: Is this a resort or a real village? How long does the journey take? What does a stay include? What conditions matter? And how do I ask about availability without making assumptions? For this business, practical clarity is not admin. It is trust.

## Slide 3 - The solution (0:40-1:00, 20s)

We turned those scattered answers into one mobile-first five-page journey. Visitors discover Long Taa on Home, understand stay options and costs, explore the place, learn the community context, then plan and open a structured WhatsApp enquiry. It is designed around the way a traveller actually decides, especially on a phone.

## Slide 4 - Live demo (1:00-2:05, 65s)

On the site, the storytelling stays anchored in authentic Long Taa photography. The stay page makes the confirmed choices clear: RM50 per person per night for accommodation only, or RM180 with breakfast, lunch, and local dinner. It explains transport, capacity, inclusions, and what is not included without inventing package totals. The explore page gives the Dapui River, Tagang conservation area, rainforest, and natural wonders context, while clearly saying activities depend on weather, road, river, water level, availability, and safety conditions. The heritage page makes the distinction explicit: Long Taa is a living village, not a staged attraction. Finally, Plan and Book gathers useful trip details into a concise WhatsApp enquiry. It begins a conversation with Clement Langet. It does not pretend a booking is confirmed.

## Slide 5 - Business value (2:05-2:29, 24s)

That clarity is the conversion strategy. The site gives Long Taa a credible digital home, makes the offer easier to share, and helps visitors arrive with more suitable expectations. It can reduce repetitive basic questions and support better-qualified enquiries, without claiming results that have not yet been measured.

## Slide 6 - Community impact and proof (2:29-2:49, 20s)

The strongest part of Long Taa is community ownership. The site frames culture as living identity, not decoration, and keeps respectful-visit guidance visible. The Borneo Post reported on 1 September 2026 that the Sebup community received the Gamuda Inspiration Award for Environment and Wildlife, and the APPGM-SDG Annual Report separately records Tagang implementation support in Dapui, Tinjar Baram.

## Slide 7 - Close (2:49-2:58, 9s)

Long Taa now has a mobile-first path from curiosity to a qualified conversation. Protect nature. Respect culture. Share our heritage. Thank you.
