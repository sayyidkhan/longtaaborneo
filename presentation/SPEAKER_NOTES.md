# Long Taa Borneo Eco Stay - Speaker Notes

Use with `SPEAKER_SCRIPT.md`. The script is timed at 178 seconds; these notes are delivery cues, not extra material to add during the pitch.

## Slide 1 - Hook

- Pause after the first line. Let the image establish remoteness.
- Say "living Sebup village" before saying "website" to keep the beneficiary central.

## Slide 2 - Challenge

- Make the tension practical: a visitor needs answers before committing to a long journey.
- Do not call the existing materials poor; say the information was fragmented.

## Slide 3 - Solution

- Point across the five-page sequence left to right.
- Emphasise mobile-first, then say the output is a qualified conversation rather than a claimed booking.

## Slide 4 - Demo

- If the live site is available, demonstrate Home, Stay & Packages, then Plan & Book in that order.
- Show one factual rate and the WhatsApp enquiry summary.
- If connectivity fails, use the slide itself and say: "The same practical journey is preserved here as our fallback."
- Do not claim an activity is guaranteed; repeat that conditions apply where relevant.

## Slide 5 - Value

- Use "can" and "supports", never assert unmeasured conversion or revenue results.
- Land on "clarity is the conversion strategy" before moving to community context.

## Slide 6 - Community

- Keep this respectful and brief: culture is living identity, not decoration.
- Attribute the award verbally only as written. Do not call it a certification or use a Gamuda logo.

## Slide 7 - Close

- Read the close slowly.
- Point to the site only if it has been verified publicly; otherwise show the PDF deck as the submission artefact.
