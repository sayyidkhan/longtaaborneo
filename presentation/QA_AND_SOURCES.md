## Likely Q&A

### Why a website instead of only social media?

Social channels are useful for discovery, but the supplied information was fragmented. A five-page website gives a shareable, practical source of truth that can explain the journey, stay options, capacities, conditions, and enquiry process in one place.

### How does this create business value without overstating results?

It can improve discoverability, reduce basic planning friction, and support better-qualified WhatsApp conversations. We do not claim bookings, conversion uplift, revenue, visitor counts, or impact metrics before they are measured.

### Is Long Taa presented as a luxury resort?

No. The content explicitly identifies it as a living Sebup village with simple longhouse accommodation. The design is polished so information is easier to use, not to misrepresent the experience.

### How do you handle pricing where package totals are not supplied?

We publish only confirmed itemised rates: RM50 accommodation-only per person per night, RM180 with local meals per person per night, RM1,500 return 4WD per vehicle, and RM600 longboat, local guide, and porter per group. We do not invent Package 1 or 2 totals. Final quotation and availability are confirmed on WhatsApp.

### How do you handle culture and conservation responsibly?

The narrative prioritises community ownership, visitor respect, condition-dependent activity wording, and a living-heritage framing. Detailed protocols, restricted locations, photography guidance, image consent, and final cultural language need beneficiary/community approval before public promotion.

### What happens if internet connectivity fails during the pitch?

The deck includes the same authentic visual story and conversion path as the site. The exported PDF can be opened locally as a reliable fallback.
